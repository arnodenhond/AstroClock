---ABOUT APP---
Help
AstroClock visualises time according to consistent astronomical standards in stead of months and years with changing amount of days.\n<b>Cycles</b>\nTime is measured according to three natural cycles:
<li><b>Day </b>\t One rotation every 24 hours. Hand at 12 o\'clock position at mid-day.</li>
<li><b>Moon</b>\t One rotation every 29.53 days. Hand at 12 o\'clock position at full moon.</li>
<li><b>Year</b>\t One rotation every 365.25 days. Hand at 12 o\'clock postion at the longest day of the year.</li>
The hands always turn in clockwise direction but the 12 o\'clock positions could be different in some themes.
<b>Sun rise and Sun set</b>\nWithin the day cylce there are indicators showing the start and end of day and night.
<b>Status Bar Notifications</b>\nBy enabling alerts you can receive up to 12 different recurring notifications, even when the screen is off.
<b>Homescreen</b>\nAstroClock also comes with a homescreen widget, a live folder and a battery efficient live wallpaper.
<b>Make your own Theme</b>\nFor instructions on creating your own theme and sharing it with others, please visit android.arnodenhond.com/apps/astroclock/skins
<b>App Interaction</b>\nAstroClock sends out broadcast intents which other apps can receive and offers a content provider which other apps can read. See android.arnodenhond.com/apps/astroclock/broadcast-intents for more information.
<b>Disclaimer</b>\nThis app does not contain spyware or otherwise misbehave in any way. Some screens are known to burn in afterimages when displaying the same graphics over a long period.

---PLAY STORE---
AstroClock takes timekeeping to an astronomical level!
Calendars divide years into months of irregular length and even the length of a year changes during leap years! AstroClock visualizes time according to three consistent natural cycles i.e. day, moon and season. Time is indicated by three hands which each turn once for each cycle. (they still turn in a clockwise direction) Bring your home screen to life with a gorgeous widget (resizable on newer phones and tablets) or a live wallpaper (very battery efficient as it ticks only once every 15 minutes). On older phones AstroClock provides a LiveFolder listing all upcoming alerts. Display AstroClock on your wall mounted tablet or wrist watch in full screen mode to view the clock without your home screen in the way.
AstroClock has a variety of downloadable themes to match your mood and if there isn't one that suits your fancy you can design your own theme, share it with others and become moderately famous! If you don't want any theme or clock to display at all you can simply enable any of the 12 alerts (4 for each cycle) and have them show up in your notification bar as desired.
AstroClock plays nicely with other apps: on each alert (whether enabled for notification or not) it broadcasts an intent to any app that wants to respond according to the astronomical time. Additionally, AstroClock offers a Content Provider that other apps can read to get the dates of all upcoming alerts.
This app is made by an independent developer during evenings and weekends. If you like it then please post a comment below :)
